ABOUT ME

// The longer version — how I embed into messy real-world problems and ship end-to-end AI systems.

I'm Md Tanvir Islam, a Forward Deployed Engineer & AI Engineer and Computer Science graduate from Bangladesh University of Business and Technology (BUBT). I work at the exact boundary where machine learning meets mission-critical operations — because training a model is only half the job, and the harder half is scoping the real problem, building the surrounding software stack, and getting it to run reliably in production.

Why Forward Deployed Engineering + AI?

It started with one question: how does a machine actually learn? Chasing that answer led me through neural networks, computer vision, and deep learning architectures. But early on I realised that a model sitting inside a Jupyter Notebook does not solve anyone's operational bottleneck.

Real organisations need an engineer who can sit with a messy, ambiguous domain problem — whether in clinical diagnostics, agricultural pathology, agency scope management, or fintech — translate it into a concrete system architecture, train and validate the AI pipeline, and ship the full-stack interface, API, database, and Docker container around it.

Today I own that entire path end-to-end: dataset engineering, model training and ablation, then the FastAPI / NestJS / Flask backend, the Next.js / React frontend, and live cloud deployment. AtlasRec (FAISS vector search + 6 ML recommenders + Next.js 15), Scopewise (rule-based agency scope & delivery workspace), and MangoCare AI (DenseNet-121 + Flask + Docker) are direct examples of that Forward Deployed Engineering mindset.

Applied AI & Research Rigor

My strongest AI research work is in medical imaging and explainable machine learning. I built a segmentation and classification pipeline for breast lesions in ultrasound images using U-Net with a ResNet-50 backbone, reaching a 91% Dice coefficient — seven percentage points above the baseline CNN. I have since worked on pneumonia triage from chest X-rays with YOLOv8 (95%+ accuracy), explainable counterfactual recommendations with FAISS, and clinical survival prediction using calibrated gradient-boosting ensembles.

In early 2026 I worked as a Research Assistant at InsightEdu across ML, deep learning, NLP and Transformer projects — reproducing state-of-the-art baselines and drafting the methodology and experimental-results sections of research papers.

WHAT DRIVES ME

HOW I OPERATE

Scope the real bottleneck

Forward deployed work starts with listening to operators and users, cutting through vague requirements, and designing the simplest architecture that solves the core problem.

Measure, then claim

A model is only as good as the number you can defend. I report Dice, RMSE, latency, and per-class metrics, including ablation runs, because engineering without numbers is guesswork.

Finish the last mile

A notebook that only runs on my machine is not a finished product. It counts once it has an interface, an API, authentication, E2E tests, and a live URL.

Explain the trade-offs

Whether speaking with clinical researchers, agency leads, or engineering teams, I make architectural trade-offs and model decisions transparent and defensible.
