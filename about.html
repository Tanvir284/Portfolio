WHO I AM

ABOUT ME

// The longer version — how I got here and what I care about.

I'm Md Tanvir Islam, a Computer Science graduate from Bangladesh University of Business and Technology, specialising in Artificial Intelligence. I work at the point where machine learning meets software that people actually use — training the model is only half the job, and the half nobody sees is getting it to run reliably behind a real interface.

My story

It started with one question: how does a machine actually learn? Chasing that answer led me through neural networks, the mathematics underneath them, and a lot of late nights working out why a model that looked correct on paper refused to converge.

Somewhere in my undergrad I realised that understanding the theory was not enough for me. I wanted to build things people could open and use. That pulled me into full-stack development — PHP and MySQL first, then C# and .NET, then React, Next.js and FastAPI — so that a trained model could become a product instead of a notebook.

Today I build across the whole path: dataset preprocessing, training and evaluation, then the API, the interface and the deployment. MangoCare AI is the clearest example — a fine-tuned DenseNet-121 wrapped in a Flask service, containerised with Docker and shipped as something you can point a camera at.

Research

My strongest research work is in medical imaging. I built a segmentation and classification pipeline for breast lesions in ultrasound images using U-Net with a ResNet-50 backbone, reaching a 91% Dice coefficient — seven percentage points above the baseline CNN. I have since worked on pneumonia classification from chest X-rays with YOLOv8, and on survival prediction using calibrated gradient-boosting ensembles.

In early 2026 I worked as a Research Assistant at InsightEdu across ML, deep learning, NLP and Transformer projects — reproducing state-of-the-art baselines and drafting the methodology and experimental-results sections of research papers.

WHAT DRIVES ME

HOW I WORK

Measure, then claim

A model is only as good as the number you can defend. I report Dice, RMSE and per-class metrics, including the runs that did not work, because a portfolio without numbers is just a list of nouns.

Finish the last mile

A notebook that only runs on my machine is not a project. It counts once it has an interface, an API and somewhere to live.

Learn in public

Currently working through Generative AI, MLOps and cloud architecture. Every repository I push is part of that record, including the rough ones.

Explain the work

Drafting research methodology taught me that unexplained work has no value. If I cannot describe why an architecture was chosen, I do not understand it yet.
